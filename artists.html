Open call

Bring us your work

Calabar Fringe welcomes submissions from artists anywhere in the world. Every entry is read by a selection panel.

Short filmFilms up to 20 minutes, completed on or after 1 January 2024, any genre. Entries are taken through FilmFreeway, and Nigerian filmmakers enter free. Final deadline 6 November 2026, with selections announced by 20 November.
TheatreNew plays up to one hour, and shorter pieces for the daily programme.
Music, comedy, poetry, danceLive acts for the daily programme.
